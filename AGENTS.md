# Karak Tea

Personal independent Horizon mod, namespace `karak_house`. Mod and signature drink: Karak Tea; buildings: Karak Houses. Target Horizon `66f3981`. Preserve the single-house-per-village design, one-emerald signature tea, and separate persistent daily giveaway. Use vanilla textures and editable geometry; no generated raster imagery. Test in disposable instances and worlds; preserve existing saves. Keep credentials, private research, and personal logs outside public commits.
