# Validation

Karak Tea 0.2.0 targets Horizon nightly `66f3981`. Testing was performed on Apple Silicon macOS, and the owner played and approved the mod before release.

The automated suite completed 45 tests: 40 passed, with five live-world tests skipped because their dedicated test fixtures were not running. Separate disposable-world tests covered the six base-price trades and drink effects, milk, seller and offer persistence, door traversal, and village generation. A sample of 25 villages contained 21 Karak Houses.

The release archive contains the same 157 gameplay files as the owner's tested installation, plus the MIT license. The tested installation SHA-256 is `73080f5de5604d8efa68bc8408a3c14b43753cb47c56bf8ab0b827333ae1fd17`.
