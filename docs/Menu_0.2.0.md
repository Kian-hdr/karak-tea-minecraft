# Karak Tea 0.2.0 menu

This preview uses the standard Horizon client.

| Drink | Base price | Duration | Powers |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Karak Tea | 1 emerald | 5 minutes | Strength IV, Speed III, Jump Boost III, Resistance II, Regeneration I, Fire Resistance I, no fall damage |
| Iced Karak | 1 emerald | 5 minutes | Fire Resistance I and Water Breathing I |
| Decaf Karak | 1 emerald | 5 minutes | Night Vision I and Regeneration I |
| Karak Milkshake | 2 emeralds | 5 minutes | Clears harmful effects when consumed, then rapidly clears Poison, Nausea and Hunger |
| Karak Protein Shake | 3 emeralds | 5 minutes | Strength VII |
| Karak Pro Max | 8 emeralds | 30 minutes | Strength VI, Speed IV, Jump Boost IV, Resistance III, Regeneration II, Fire Resistance I, no fall damage |

One payment buys one serving. Each drink contributes once. Another serving of the same drink refreshes only its timer. Different drinks stack their contributions and expire independently. Overlapping effect levels add: regular Strength IV plus Pro Max Strength VI gives Strength X until one of those drinks expires. Binary protections remain active while at least one contributing drink remains active.

The Milkshake keeps beneficial Karak powers. Ordinary Minecraft milk clears all effects and Karak timers. Fall protection keeps normal falling speed; Slow Falling is removed.

Each drink has its own design: the signature square glass jar, tall iced tumbler with straw, handled Decaf cup, Milkshake cup and straw, Protein shaker, and taller oversized Pro Max jar. The occasional free cup remains regular Karak Tea.

This pack uses Horizon's standard villager trade screen. Its second ingredient slot remains visible and empty; copper cannot pay. Sellers wear a cream cap and the vanilla butcher apron. Hero of the Village can discount the higher-priced drinks. On the standard client, Poison can cause an initial damage tick before the Milkshake clears it.
